## 1. Ministry of Commerce: Digital Business Identification (DBID) data

**To:** [Digital Commerce Cell / DBID team, Ministry of Commerce; address from the official mincom.gov.bd or DBID portal]
**Subject:** Request for access to registered DBID business data (aggregate or opt-in list)

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am [name], [role] at [company/organisation], based in [city]. We are building a business-location data service for Bangladesh
that helps companies understand where retail, pharmacy, education and online businesses operate. It is built only from open data
(OpenStreetMap, Overture Maps) and sources used with permission.

F-commerce businesses are hard to represent fairly because most have no shop address. DBID registration is the most reliable
official record of them. We would like to ask:

1. Whether a list of DBID-registered businesses (business name, business page/website link, business category, district) can be
   shared or published, for example as open data;
2. If individual records cannot be shared, whether **aggregate counts** by district and category are available;
3. Whether registered businesses could **opt in** to being listed in public directories.

We do not request owners' personal details (NID, personal phone, home address). We would follow any conditions the Ministry sets,
credit the Ministry as the source, and can share our coverage findings with the Ministry in return.

Thank you for your time. I am happy to meet or provide more detail.

Kind regards,
[name] · [phone] · [email] · [company registration / trade licence no., if any]

is for academic and non-profit **research**, not commercial products. A commercial request is likely to be refused, so only send
this if you have a genuine research partner (e.g. a university) or want to ask about a commercial data partnership.

**To:** [use the application form linked from Meta's Ad Library API / Meta Content Library pages, or a Meta for Business partner contact]
**Subject:** Data access enquiry: commercial advertisers in Bangladesh (Ad Library)

Hello,

I am [name] from [company / university partner]. We study the online (F-commerce) business sector in Bangladesh, where most small
sellers operate only through Facebook pages. The Ad Library shows commercial advertisers in Bangladesh on its website, but the
API currently covers only political and social-issue ads for this country.

We would like to ask whether there is an approved way to access **page-level advertiser information** (page name, page link, page
category, country) for commercial advertisers in Bangladesh, either through the API, the Meta Content Library, or a data
partnership. Our purpose is [research on the size and location of the F-commerce sector / a business directory]; we do not need
ad content, user data or any personal information.

Thank you,
[name] · [organisation] · [email]
